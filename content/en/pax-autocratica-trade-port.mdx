# Pax Autocratica Trade Port

After AG-25, treat the Trade Port as a useful but still changing base system, not a solved passive-income machine. The official AG-25 note says a broader Trade Port update is already in progress, specifically around risky items sold to soldiers and requests to let Leaders sell items directly. Current player discussions also connect the building to money pressure and unwanted soldier effects. Use it carefully, check soldier logs, and do not assume a direct sell-from-Warehouse feature exists until a dated patch note confirms it.

## What AG-25 confirms

Directive AG-25 is the current official update boundary for this page. It confirms many base and quality-of-life changes, including better production scheduling, improved soldier hauling, automatic returns for some harvested outputs, and a new Colony Difficulty Customization screen.

For the Trade Port, the important line is not a completed fix. The update says the team is aware of current Trade Port issues, including questionable items being sold to soldiers, and that a broader Trade Port update is already being worked on. That means the topic is real, but the final design is not yet documented.

1. Use AG-25 as proof that Trade Port issues are on the official radar.
2. Do not treat the planned broader update as already shipped.
3. Separate Trade Port questions from unrelated hauling or Warehouse problems.

## Why players are asking about it

One active Steam discussion asks how to handle daily money pressure and whether the Trade Port can sell a player's own ores. Replies suggest expedition income and item selling routes, but the thread does not establish an official passive-income rule.

Another large discussion connects Trade Port purchases to sudden negative traits and asks for ways to restrict or blacklist items. A separate current hub discussion asks the developers to preserve some of the Trade Port's chaotic story value while improving the system.

1. If your problem is money, test expedition rewards and current trader interactions before assuming passive sales exist.
2. If your problem is soldier behavior, inspect the soldier log before blaming aging, morale, or a hidden random event.
3. Keep one note with the build date, Trade Port status, soldier log entry, and any item involved.

## A safe checklist before building around it

The safest planning stance is conservative: the Trade Port may help your run, but it is still under active Early Access revision. A page that promises a universal money route or a complete item blacklist would be too strong for today's evidence.

Use the building when you can tolerate some uncertainty, and keep your economy from depending on an unconfirmed future update. If a soldier suddenly changes state, look for purchase history or other direct evidence before deleting buildings, changing colony settings, or assuming your save is broken.

1. Check AG-25 or a newer official update before relying on any Trade Port guide.
2. Read the building text and soldier logs in your current build.
3. Avoid making the Trade Port your only money plan until direct selling is officially documented.
4. Use the official Steam discussion or Discord route for reproducible reports.

## What this page will not claim

This page does not publish a complete Trade Port item table, a guaranteed way to sell every ore from storage, or a fixed list of soldier-purchase side effects. The current evidence supports a caution page and a reporting checklist, not a full mechanics database.

The next useful update to this page should come from a dated official patch note that names Trade Port changes, or from repeatable current-build tests with enough detail to separate intended behavior from bugs.

## FAQ

### Can the Trade Port sell my own ores automatically?

Not as a confirmed AG-25 rule. Players are asking about direct selling, and AG-25 says a broader Trade Port update is in progress, but it does not document a finished direct-sell feature.

### Can Trade Port purchases affect soldiers?

Player reports connect Trade Port purchases to unwanted effects, and AG-25 names risky soldier purchases as an area under review. Check the soldier log in your current build before drawing a conclusion.

### Should I delete the Trade Port?

Do not treat deletion as a universal fix. First confirm your current issue, check logs, and compare against the latest official update because the system is actively being revised.
