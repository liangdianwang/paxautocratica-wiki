# Pax Autocratica Resource Transport Station Warehouse

If your Resource Transport Station or transport hub is upgraded and the resource icon or stored contents stop showing in the station, first check the Warehouse before calling it a bug. Two current community threads point to the same practical explanation: once the station reaches the relevant upgraded state, resources may feed directly into the Warehouse, so there may be nothing left to collect manually at the station. This page treats that as a player-reported checklist, not an official patch note. If the Warehouse count does not change, record your build, station level, worker assignment, and route before reporting it.

## What the current reports show

A Steam discussion describes the friction clearly: resources were sitting in the transport hub and had to be picked up manually, even after a Warehouse existed. The useful reply in that thread says to upgrade the transport hub, and later comments point to level 2 wording as the place where the automatic behavior is explained.

A separate Reddit thread from the current watch window reports the same symptom from the other side: after upgrading the Resource Transporting Station to level 2, the visible contents disappeared. Replies there say Mk2 feeds directly into the Warehouse, so the station may look empty because it is no longer the pickup point.

1. Treat the missing station contents as a Warehouse-flow check first, not as proof of lost resources.
2. Use both threads as player-reported evidence, not as a final official mechanics table.
3. Keep the checked build and date with your own notes because Pax Autocratica is still in Early Access.

## Quick checklist before reporting a bug

The safest test is simple: pause long enough to record the Warehouse amount, let a worker finish a resource trip, then check whether the Warehouse increases while the station stays empty. If the Warehouse rises, the likely explanation is direct transfer from the upgraded station.

Also check whether the building is actually upgraded to the level discussed in the player reports. The wording may appear in the build or tooltip surface rather than in the upgrade confirmation itself, which is why players can miss it.

1. Confirm that a Warehouse exists and is reachable.
2. Confirm the transport building level and read its current tooltip or building detail text.
3. Watch one worker delivery cycle instead of checking only the station icon.
4. Compare Warehouse inventory before and after the delivery.
5. If inventory rises, stop manually emptying the transport station.
6. If inventory does not rise, capture the build date, worker assignment, station level, resource type, and route for a bug report.

## When it is probably not the same issue

Do not merge every storage problem into the Mk2 Warehouse explanation. If workers are idle, routes are blocked, the Warehouse is full, a different building is waiting for a specific resource, or a tutorial objective is stuck, you may be dealing with another problem.

The Steam store and official news make the broader context clear: this is an Early Access game with many interacting systems and active feedback-driven updates. That makes dated checks more reliable than old snippets, and it means a community workaround should remain scoped to the exact symptom it describes.

1. Do not assume all missing icons mean resources were transferred.
2. Do not install third-party fixes or trainers to solve a hauling question.
3. Use Steam Discussions or the official community routes if you can reproduce a real loss.

## FAQ

### Did my Resource Transport Station delete the resources?

Not necessarily. If the building is upgraded, first check whether the Warehouse count increased while the station looked empty.

### Is the Mk2 Warehouse behavior officially documented here?

No. This page uses current player reports as a checklist and keeps the official boundary separate.

### What should I report if the Warehouse does not receive anything?

Record the build date, transport building level, Warehouse status, worker assignment, resource type, and a short reproduction path.
